# 奥利 Ollie the Otter｜角色设定三案

三案均为原创幼儿英语启蒙角色探索稿：左侧全身正面站姿，右侧依次为开心、好奇表情。统一采用手工黏土定格动画质感、可见的轻微指纹纹理、柔和暖光与浅米色纯色背景；无文字、无水印。以下 Hex 为从成图局部取样的**参考色**，受光照影响，不等同于精确材质色卡。后续制作应以对应图片的脸型、比例和配饰为首要参照。

## A｜经典圆滚滚

- **造型**：大圆头、短四肢、饱满梨形躯干；奶油色大肚皮与口鼻，深棕鼻头，棕色短须；无服饰。尾巴从身体侧后方伸出。整体亲近、稳定，适合做系列基础款。
- **参考配色**：主体暖栗棕 `#B7653B`；口鼻及腹部奶油杏 `#F7CC9C`；鼻头深可可 `#4D260F`；背景浅米色 `#FDE4C8`。
- **适合的动作与表情**：拍手、挥手、抱住单词卡、笨拙地小跳；开心时张口笑、好奇时抬眉并轻张圆嘴。短肢动作宜做大幅度轮廓变化。
- **优点**：形体简洁，正面辨识与逐帧复现容易；没有配饰连续性负担。
- **缺点**：纯棕加奶油腹部的组合较常见，后续需要靠固定脸部比例、语气和动作建立独特记忆点。
- **英文一致性提示词**：

  > Original preschool mascot Ollie the Otter, design A. A very round, chubby, pear-shaped little otter with an oversized round head, tiny rounded ears, short thick arms and legs, a broad cream-apricot muzzle and oval belly, warm chestnut-brown body, small dark-cocoa oval nose, two short dark-brown clay whiskers on each cheek, large dark eyes and a thick tail extending behind to the viewer's left. No clothes or accessories. Curious, eager to imitate, gently clumsy. Keep the exact head-to-body ratio, muzzle outline, belly size, colors and whisker count consistent with the reference sheet. Show [POSE] and [EXPRESSION] as a full-body handmade plasticine stop-motion character with subtle fingerprint impressions, tiny sculpting irregularities, soft warm studio lighting, a solid pale-beige background, and a soft ground shadow. Child-friendly for ages 3–6; original design, no text, no watermark.

## B｜浅棕红围巾

- **造型**：比 A 更高、更纤细，四肢略长，动作空间更大；蜂蜜浅棕身体、奶油色口鼻与腹部，红色黏土编织感围巾是固定识别件。大眼、深棕鼻与短须保留水獭特征。
- **参考配色**：主体蜂蜜浅棕 `#E18D45`；口鼻及腹部奶油色 `#EFC194`；围巾珊瑚红 `#E5514A`；背景浅米色 `#FDE1C7`。
- **适合的动作与表情**：张臂模仿老师、指向画面物品、踮脚探看、转身时围巾轻摆；开心时眯眼大笑，好奇时歪头、睁大眼睛并形成小圆嘴。
- **优点**：红围巾形成明确色彩锚点；较长四肢利于表演指示与模仿动作。
- **缺点**：围巾编织纹理和垂挂方向需要逐帧统一；冬季联想较强，夏日或水边场景需处理搭配逻辑。
- **英文一致性提示词**：

  > Original preschool mascot Ollie the Otter, design B. A light honey-tan otter with a slightly tall, slim soft pear-shaped body, a round head, small rounded ears, longer flexible arms and legs, a long thick tail extending behind to the viewer's left, an ivory-cream muzzle and long oval belly, a small warm-chocolate nose, three short dark-brown clay whiskers on each cheek and expressive dark eyes. Always wear the same small coral-red chunky clay-knit scarf, wrapped once around the neck with one visible short tail hanging at the front; keep its color, wrap and hanging direction consistent in every pose. Curious, eager to imitate, gently clumsy. Keep the reference sheet's silhouette, face geometry, eye spacing and scarf proportions. Show [POSE] and [EXPRESSION] as a full-body handmade plasticine stop-motion character with subtle fingerprint impressions, tiny sculpting irregularities, soft warm studio lighting, a solid pale-beige background, and a soft ground shadow. Child-friendly for ages 3–6; original design, no text, no watermark.

## C｜深棕黄背带裤

- **造型**：比 B 矮壮、肩身更宽，深棕身体配焦糖色口鼻；明黄色短背带裤有两条背带、两个圆扣和胸前口袋。脸颊的浅色短须与明黄服饰形成醒目对比。
- **参考配色**：主体深棕 `#8B412A`；口鼻焦糖色 `#E58D48`；背带裤奶油黄 `#FABD3D`；背景浅米色 `#FCE4CC`。
- **适合的动作与表情**：双手叉腰、从胸前口袋掏出字母卡、蹲下观察、尝试新动作时轻微绊脚；开心时眯眼开怀笑，好奇时睁大眼并噘成小圆嘴。
- **优点**：深棕与黄色形成最强的远景对比；背带裤、圆扣和口袋可支撑节目中的道具互动。
- **缺点**：服装细节最多，逐帧制作成本更高；口袋与背带在转身、举手和蹲下时容易失真。
- **英文一致性提示词**：

  > Original preschool mascot Ollie the Otter, design C. A compact sturdy little otter with a large rounded head, short broad pear-shaped torso, short legs and paws, small round ears, a thick tail extending behind to the viewer's left, deep espresso-brown body, warm caramel muzzle and belly, small dark-brown nose, two short cream-colored clay whiskers on each cheek, and wide dark eyes. Always wear the same buttery-yellow clay short overalls: two broad shoulder straps, one round yellow button on each strap, a centered bib pocket, and rolled short hems. Keep the overalls' cut, pocket, button count and yellow hue consistent in every pose. Curious, eager to imitate, gently clumsy. Keep the reference sheet's head-to-body ratio, face geometry and compact silhouette. Show [POSE] and [EXPRESSION] as a full-body handmade plasticine stop-motion character with subtle fingerprint impressions, tiny sculpting irregularities, soft warm studio lighting, a solid pale-beige background, and a soft ground shadow. Child-friendly for ages 3–6; original design, no text, no watermark.

## 后续统一约束

将各版英文提示词中的 `[POSE]`、`[EXPRESSION]` 替换为具体动作和表情；同一版始终附对应设定图作为视觉参考。不要在同一套姿势中混用 A/B/C 的体型、配饰、须色或腹部形状。先固定正面、侧面、背面与三视图，再扩展动作，以减少跨镜头漂移。
